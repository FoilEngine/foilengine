# ADR 0015 — Free project under the Legal Jibber Jabber policy

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: 2026-10-30

## Context

Foil uses Riot assets (cards, illustrations, card backs) without a commercial license.

## Decision

The project stays free: no paywall, subscription, crowdfunding or commercial entity. The official notice appears on the website and in the application. No Riot logo or trademark in Foil's identity.

## Consequences

Riot can ask for the project to stop at any time: users must be able to export their decks and replays.
